    Research Case Studies

    Published in JCTC (2025)

    Direction-Guided Rare-Event Adaptive Sampling

    How can rare transition pathways be generated without becoming trapped in deep metastable basins?

    Method & contribution

    Developed PathGennie, which directs short parallel molecular-dynamics segments using real-time progress-coordinate decisions.

    Supported result

    On monitored benchmark systems, complete pathways were constructed from 50–100 ps segments with 10–100× faster pathway discovery, without changing the physical potential.

    PaperCode
    Published in JCTC (2025)

    Unsupervised Structural Phase Identification

    How can metastable states and structural phases be identified directly from molecular trajectories?

    Method & contribution

    Developed IceCoder by coupling SOAP local-density descriptors with a variational autoencoder for low-dimensional structural representations.

    Supported result

    Separated hexagonal ice, cubic ice, and liquid-water environments in latent space and tracked phase-boundary nucleation.

    PaperCode
    Open-source research software

    Lineage-Aware Adaptive Conformational Sampling

    How can adaptive sampling preserve continuous physical trajectory histories?

    Method & contribution

    Designed TRAILS-MD, an engine-agnostic adaptive-sampling framework with density-aware seed selection and explicit trajectory-lineage tracking.

    Supported result

    Reconstructs continuous conformational routes from parallel short trajectory fragments, avoiding pathway fragmentation artifacts.

    Research Statement

    I develop interpretable, physics-grounded computational methods at the intersection of molecular dynamics, enhanced sampling, and machine learning. My work addresses a recurring bottleneck in molecular simulation: bridging the gap between the femtosecond timestep of atomic forces and the micro- to millisecond timescales at which biologically and chemically relevant events — conformational changes, nucleation, ligand binding — actually occur.

    During my Ph.D., I built methods that accelerate this process from both ends: representation learning to identify reaction coordinates and structural phases directly from trajectory data (IceCoder), adaptive sampling to steer short parallel simulations toward rare transition pathways (PathGennie, TRAILS-MD), and kinetics estimation through weighted-ensemble resampling with temporal coherence (CoWERA).

    I am motivated by problems where data-driven models must respect physical symmetries, conservation laws, and thermodynamic consistency — not just minimise loss functions. My postdoctoral research vision focuses on physics-aware molecular representation learning, scalable adaptive sampling for drug discovery, and trajectory-resolved kinetics for complex biomolecular systems.
